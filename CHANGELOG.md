## Suggested future entry types

When the project grows, future entries may use headings like:

- **Added** — new files, folders, scripts, or documentation
- **Changed** — updates to structure, wording, or workflow
- **Clarified** — corrected assumptions or better definitions
- **Verified** — a finding moved from tentative to confirmed
- **Deprecated** — old approaches no longer recommended
- **Removed** — things intentionally deleted or replaced
